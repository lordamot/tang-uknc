Item 1 is done.

Upstream issue #31 is the dc_fl bug in the VM2 predecoder.  dc_fl was
formed from the registered copies of the exchange-type flags that choose
the bus operation - R, W or RMW - and those are predecoder outputs that
can change on the same edge as dc_fl itself, so in a synchronous model it
latched the previous clock's state.  ix[0], the RMW flag, is loaded from
it, so an inc (PC)+ following an instruction that had set the exchange
type to read - upstream's test uses cmp #1, #1 - read the prefetch
register instead of doing the read-modify-write.

It was fixed upstream on 22 August 2026, commit e057663, "vm2: fix
instruction pre-decoder dc_mop flags", which is four days after the issue
and was not there when this repository's copy was taken.  The patch
touches one line in vm2_wb.v:

  -assign dc_fl = (ir_stb ? pld[10] : ~dc_fb) & ((ir_stb & dc_bi) | (dc_iord & ~dc_iowr));
  +assign dc_fl = ir_stb ? pld[10] & (pld[5] | (pld[4] & ~pld[3])) :
  +                        ~dc_fb & (dc_iord & ~dc_iowr);

The ir_stb branch now reads the predecoder outputs directly instead of
their registered copies.  Applied verbatim to tang/src/wm2wb/vm2_wb.v with
a comment recording where it came from and why.  It is Verilog, not VHDL -
the VHDL in this tree is the unused AY line under tang/src/ay/.

Both processors are this same module, so it lands on the CPU and the PPU
alike.  Lint is clean and the machine boots in simulation with the timings
unchanged, which is the regression that matters.  It does not change the
two open oddities from the last exchange: the read-after-write mismatches
that start at 1.037 s and the PPU halting the CPU at 1.047 s are both
still exactly where they were.

Our copy of the core is from October 2023 and still predates three other
upstream fixes: 8d097ae (Aug 2024, a non-blocking assignment in the
prefetch), and abe8b68 and 2f765b4 (Dec 2023, shadow PSW/PC in halt mode,
and the interrupt-acknowledge timeout vector).  None applied - say if they
should be.
