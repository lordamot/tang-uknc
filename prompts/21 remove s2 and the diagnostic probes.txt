What went, in top.v:

- The DC blocker and its S2 bypass.  dc_acc, dc_mean, dc_out and
  dc_amp = buts[1] ? dc_out : dc_x are gone; the volume stage now takes
  snd_amp = $signed({2'b00, mix}) and nothing else.  clip() stays, because
  mix reaches 34744 and a signed sample stops at 32767.  buts[1] is read
  nowhere now - the port stays two bits wide and pin 87 stays constrained,
  so S2 is present and does nothing.
- The whole diagnostics block: the dbgmon instance, the eighteen probe
  words, the accumulators and snapshots behind them, and the three
  hdmi_audio_* counter connections that only fed them.  About 160 lines.
  uart_tx is assign uart_tx = vp65_uart_tx again, so pin 69 is the UKNC's
  own C2 line.
- src/dbg/dbgmon.v and tools/dbgmon.py both stay.  The module is in the
  .gprj and instantiated nowhere, like ip/dvi_tx; putting it back is that
  one instance plus a probes list.
