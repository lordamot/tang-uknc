Rework the audio mixing so that the output level is bounded by construction
rather than by summing, and so that the peak sample is 2040 whatever is
playing.

Why, so the numbers are not arbitrary.  Measured on the operator's
television, 1 Sep 2026, by walking a test tone up a level ladder and
listening at each volume setting.  The sample values are what reached the
HDMI encoder:

  2040   plays cleanly  (seen twice, from two different sources)
  3060   plays
  4080   plays but audibly attenuated
  6120   sounds briefly, then silence  (seen twice, two different sources)
 12240   sounds briefly, then silence

The knee is a property of the sink, it is absolute rather than relative to
the content, and it is repeatable across different combinations of sources
producing the same level.  2040 is therefore chosen as the ceiling: it is
the highest level that played cleanly every time it was tried.  The
attenuation is believed to track the mean of a unipolar signal, which the
volume stage scales along with the swing, so a louder setting moves toward
the failure rather than away from it.

WHAT TO BUILD

1.  Beeper level becomes 0 or 510, replacing 0 or 8192.

2.  Inside aberrant.v, the three channels of each AY are combined by a
    composite that saturates, not by a sum, and the three chips are then
    combined the same way.  Every one of aberrant's outputs - m_channel,
    l_channel and r_channel - carries that same composite.  The panning is
    gone; mono to both sides is what the real module does.

3.  In top.v, the AY composite and the beeper are combined by the same
    composite.

4.  The volume shift stays exactly as it is - mute, >>2, >>1, none - and is
    applied to the composite.  2040 at 100% therefore becomes 510 at 33%.

DEFINITION OF THE COMPOSITE

Let FS = 2040 be full scale.  For two sources a and b, each in 0..FS:

    composite(a, b) = a + b - (a * b) / FS

Properties that must hold, and that the tests below must check:

  composite(0, 0)   = 0
  composite(a, 0)   = a        a source alone passes through at its own level
  composite(FS, FS) = FS       everything at once is still full scale
  commutative and associative, so the order of the tree does not matter

Consequences that are the point of the exercise:

  one AY channel at amplitude 15         2040
  two AY channels at amplitude 15        2040
  three AY channels at amplitude 15      2040
  all nine channels of all three chips   2040
  beeper alone                            510
  beeper plus everything else            2040

Implementation is open, but two notes.  The multiply can be done in the
AY's native 0..255 domain and scaled by 8 at the end, which is much cheaper
than nine 12x12 multiplies in the 0..2040 domain.  And the beeper stage
needs no multiplier at all: 510/2040 is exactly 1/4, so

    composite(ay, beeper on) = ay - (ay >> 2) + 510

is exact at both ends.  Whatever is chosen, state the rounding error: the
corners above must be exact, and a lone source must pass through within
1 LSB.

CONSTRAINTS

- The composite must be REGISTERED before hdmi_tx latches it.  The sample
  register feeding volume_data_l/volume_data_r and hdmi_audio_l/r exists
  because a combinational mixer was being sampled at an instant unrelated
  to the mixer's clock, and the encoder sent carry-chain intermediates as
  samples - the audio path read as dead for three build cycles.  Adding a
  multiply tree in front of that register is the same hazard, larger.  Put
  a flop after the composite, and pipeline the tree if the depth warrants
  it; there are ~65 PPU clocks per 48 kHz sample, so latency is free.

- The ceiling must be enforced by the composite, not by clip().  clip()
  becomes unreachable at these levels; keep it as a guard and say so, but
  nothing may depend on it firing.

- No new clock domain.  PRIMARY and LW clock resources are both 8/8.

- Any new .v file must go into tang/test003.gprj.  A sim-only testbench
  goes under sim/ and must NOT go into the .gprj.

- Do not touch S2, and do not add any test-tone injector.  The machine's
  own sound is the only thing that should reach the mixer.

- Octal stays octal where the sources use it; existing Russian comments
  stay as they are.

- Fix the arithmetic in the comments around the mixer while you are in
  there.  top.v currently carries three different beeper levels in
  adjacent comments - 4096 ("the total is 22456"), 8192 (the code), and
  16384 ("0..34744") - none of them consistent with each other.  After
  this change the only correct statement is that the output is 0..2040.

THE COMPOSITE

aberrant.v combines its nine channels in the AY's own 0..255 domain, four
stages deep, one flop per stage, then scales by 8 - 255 * 8 = 2040 exactly.
Eight 8x8 multiplies rather than eight 12x12.  Stage 1 is A o B per chip
with C delayed to match, stage 2 is o C per chip, stage 3 is chip1 o chip2
with chip3 delayed, stage 4 is o chip3, stage 5 is the output register.

a*b/255 is rounded to nearest by t = p + 128; (t + (t >> 8)) >> 8.  I
checked that identity against round(p/255) for every p in 0..65025 and
against the composite over all 65536 input pairs:

  composite(a, 0) = a           exact, all 256 values
  composite(255, 255) = 255     exact
  never exceeds 255             checked exhaustively, so the 11-bit
                                l_channel and r_channel ports still fit
  error against the real-valued composite   at most 0.5 LSB
  associativity                 holds to within 1 LSB, not exactly

So the corners the specification names are exact and a lone source passes
through with no error at all, not merely within 1 LSB.

The beeper stage in top.v is ay - (ay >> 2) + 510, written as a multiply by
a Q16 constant so that retuning BEEP_LEVEL retunes the composite with it;
at 510/2040 that constant is 16384 and the synthesiser reduces it to a
shift, so the stage costs no multiplier.  Its error is not merely small, it
is zero: floor(ay * 510 / 2040) equals ay >> 2 for every one of the 2041
values the tree can produce, which the testbench checks one by one.

AUDIO_FS = 2040 and BEEP_LEVEL = 510 are localparams in top.v.  At any
ratio other than FS/4 the beeper stage costs one 12x16 multiply and is
accurate to 1 LSB.

REGISTERING

Five flops in aberrant and one more in top.v - mix_r on posedge ppuclk_p -
between the composite and the volume stage, which is itself the existing
sample register.  So what hdmi_tx latches is three registers behind the
last multiply - mono_channel, mix_r, volume_data_l - and nothing it can
reach is settling.  Seven clocks end to end against about 65 PPU clocks per
48 kHz sample: 2.2 us, and it costs nothing.

clip() is kept and is now unreachable: mix_r is 0..2040 by construction and
the volume stage only shifts down.  The comment says so.

WHAT THE TESTBENCH CHECKS

sim/tb/tb_mix.v, run by make mix-test.  Not in test003.gprj.  It drives
aberrant.v over the wishbone port the way ppu.v does - word write latches a
register number, byte write sends data - and programs R7 = 077 so tone and
noise are off and the channels hold a steady level instead of oscillating.

  silence                                       0
  A alone at amplitude 15                    2040
  A and B at amplitude 15                    2040
  A, B and C at amplitude 15                 2040
  one chip saturated                         2040
  three chips, nine channels                 2040
  l_channel follows                          2040
  r_channel follows                          2040
  A alone at amplitude 8                      320
  A alone at amplitude 12                    1152
  A and B at amplitude 8                      592
  A at 12, B at 8                            1288
  the same two levels, two chips             1288
  beeper stage vs definition, mismatches        0
  beeper alone                                510
  beeper plus saturated AY                   2040

The expected values are computed inside the testbench from the AY's own
volume table and from the definition of the composite with a real integer
divide, not from the shift-and-add the RTL uses, so the testbench and the
design cannot agree by sharing a mistake.  That is the lesson from the
SDRAM model and from the audio subframe layout, both of which agreed with
the thing they were testing.

The last line but two matters on its own: the same two levels give 1288
whether they are on one chip or spread across two, which is associativity
holding through the tree.

THE ONE THING NOT CHECKED IN RTL, AND WHY

The beeper stage lives in top.v, which the testbench cannot instantiate
without the whole machine.  So it is covered the other way round: the exact
composite is evaluated over all 2041 possible AY levels and compared with
the arithmetic top.v performs, which gives 0 mismatches and never exceeds
2040.  That checks the arithmetic.  The claim that top.v computes that
arithmetic is a code-reading claim, and I am stating it as one rather than
letting a passing test imply more than it covers.

RESOURCES

The place-and-route numbers in the prompt - Logic 9048, Register 3934 - are
not the ones in the repository.  The committed report, git show
HEAD:tang/impl/pnr/test003.rpt.txt, says 8923 and 3873, and that is the
only baseline this host can verify, so the delta is measured against it.

                     before        after
  Logic          8923/20736    9213/20736    44% -> 45%
    LUT / ALU      8264/575      8362/779
  Register       3873/15915    3949/15915    25% -> 25%
  BSRAM              22/46         22/46     48%, unchanged
  DSP                 0/24          2/24     8 x MULT9X9
  setup violations        0             0
  hold violations       559           557

+290 logic cells and +76 registers, and the eight multiplies cost none of
them: all eight went into two DSP macros, the first use this design has
made of DSP at all.  There are 24.  The extra ALU is the composite's adds
and subtracts, the extra registers are the pipeline.

Setup violations stay at 0.  The 557 hold violations are the standing
artefact - clk_25 and clk_3_12 have to be declared as independent clocks
because the SDC parser will not take create_generated_clock with a PLL-pin
source, so the tool cannot relate two clocks that are the same counter.
The number means nothing until that is fixed.

LEVELS, AND THE TWO THINGS TO FLAG

Full scale 2040 is -24.1 dBFS against a 16-bit sample.

                    full scale            beeper
  volume 100%    2040, -24.1 dBFS     510, -36.1 dBFS
  volume 66%     1020, -30.1 dBFS     255, -42.2 dBFS
  volume 33%      510, -36.2 dBFS     127, -48.2 dBFS

The beeper loses 24.1 dB exactly - 8192 to 510.  At 33% it is 127, and the
beeper at 2048 was already recorded as inaudible at that setting, so a
program whose only sound is the beeper is likely to be silent at the lower
two settings and marginal at the top one.  That is a real regression and it
is not hidden anywhere: BEEP_LEVEL is a localparam, the dBFS table is in
platform.md and in progress.md defect 11, and raising it is one edit.

Full scale at -24 dBFS makes the machine very quiet on any sink without
this television's knee.  platform.md says that in those words rather than
leaving 2040 looking like a natural constant, and AUDIO_FS is the localparam
to change.
