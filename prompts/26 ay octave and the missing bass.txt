Found and fixed, and it was not the HDMI path: every AY tone was an octave low.

The cause is one pin. aberrant.v tied the three YM2149 cores' SEL high. In ym2149.sv SEL selects the prescaler: 0 divides the clock enable by 8, which is what a real AY does (tone = fclk / (16 x period)), and 1 divides by 16, meant for a core fed at twice the chip clock - which is how the MiSTer ZX Spectrum drives it, a 3.5 MHz enable for a 1.7734 MHz chip. Here the enable is the chip clock itself, 1.7734 MHz, so every tone counter and the envelope generator ran at half rate.

Measured in a standalone simulation of the core, then in the unit test: with SEL=1, tone period 252 gives 220 Hz and an envelope of period 1000 gives 3.5 Hz; with SEL=0 they give 440 Hz and 6.9 Hz, which is what the chip does. A PT3 bass line at 65-130 Hz an octave down is 33-65 Hz, which a television's speaker cannot reproduce, while the rest of the tune an octave down still sounds like music. That is the "bass filtered out". Nothing in the HDMI path shapes frequency: the encoder takes the mixer word as it is, the resampler is a sample-and-hold, and the DC blocker is still out. The AY core itself is fine and does not need replacing; the flat tables and mixer are the same ones MiSTer plays with.

It also corrects the earlier record. The "1.77 times too fast, nine semitones sharp" figure from when CE was tied high was computed without this pin; through the divide-by-16 it was actually 12% flat. The phase accumulator was still right, it just was not the last step.

Changed: tang/src/aberrant.v - SEL low on all three instances, with the reasoning in a comment and the stale figure corrected. sim/tb/tb_aberrant.v - now counts the output's edges over one simulated second and fails outside 2% of 880, so a wrong prescaler cannot pass make ab-test again; "it oscillates" was not a pitch check. Docs: platform.md, progress.md (defect 12), and a CLAUDE.md trap.

Verified: make ab-test passes at 440 Hz, make lint clean (the one MULTIDRIVEN warning is the pre-existing one on mount_dsk in top.v), make bitstream builds - logic 44%, registers 25%, BSRAM 48%, 0 setup violations, same as before - and bin/tang.fs is the new one. What defect 4 means. A timing tool checks every path from one clock edge to the next, and it can only do that for nets it knows are clocks. This design had flops clocked by things that are not clocks: a PPU register bit (step), one-cycle pulses out of other flops (clk_dsk, clk_dsk_n, sd_rd, sd_img_mounted), a compare (curs_set). Such a "clock" travels on general routing with whatever delay the placement gives it, the SDC could only call it a made-up 1 us clock, and nothing checked it. The paths were not wrong, they were untested, and each place-and-route re-rolled them. On top of that the MCU's 20 MHz SPI clock on m0s[3] was not declared at all, so the MISO path - the one the power-on handshake and every SD mount goes through - was never analysed either. Those two together are why a re-layout could take the start screen with an SD card in: if the handshake fails the MCU never releases cold reset, and no start screen means no floppy.

Done:
- fdd4.v runs entirely on the 25 MHz clock now. clk_dsk and clk_dsk_n are enables, the track ROM is clocked by clk_25 with the pulse as its chip enable, read_sd's asynchronous clear is synchronous, and step goes through two flops and an edge detector. step was the worst one: it changes on the PPU clock, which is the same counter as clk_25, so it moved at the very edge of the flop it clocked.
- top.v samples sd_img_mounted[n] on mist_clk instead of using it as a clock. sdram2.v's cursor flop runs on clkram.
- test003.sdc drops the three invented clocks and declares spi_clk at 50 ns with input and output delays against the MCU's sampling edge, in its own asynchronous group. The report now analyses it: Fmax 31.6 MHz against the 20 MHz needed.

Verified: make fdd-test, a new bench that pulls the old fdd4 out of git and runs it beside the new one - the words the PPU reads, the sectors requested and the head position agree across a spin, stepping past track 0, an empty drive and a motor restart. make lint clean. make sim boots identically to the unmodified tree, 15 frames at 300 ms; the one read-after-write miss it reports is present on the unmodified tree too, at the same time and address, and is noted as an open question. make bitstream: 0 setup violations, 44% logic, 48% BSRAM; the timing report's clock table no longer contains any data-signal clock. Not run on a board - bin/tang.fs is the new build but the board still has this morning's; flashing it is make flash-fpga-flash and a power cycle.

Still open under defect 4: clk_25 and clk_3_12 being counter bits (the hold count, 593 this build, is that artefact), and the PPU timer, beeper divider and I2S FIFO strobe still clocked by data inside the PPU domain - slow, and not on the boot or floppy path.
