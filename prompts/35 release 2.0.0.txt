What would go out untested on hardware (per CHANGELOG.md "Unreleased")
- Sound mixer: ABC stereo, DC blocker, 4.87 kHz low-pass and the anti-alias filter. It is built and meets timing, but nobody has listened to it.

- Tang Ultima additions: the flash writer, the UART and the reconfig pin. The flash writer and the core switch were shown working on 13 Sep. The reconfig_n pin does nothing unless a wire runs from pin 48 to TP1.
