So the InfoFrame cadence was the cause.  It also retroactively confirms the
beeper fix: the AND-of-all-five really was the bug, it was just inaudible
through a sink that was not playing anything.

On the black screen, the answers that mattered were that the start screen
appears with the SD card OUT, and is absent with it in whether or not a
.dsk is mounted - and that it went away in the build that added the tone.

Nothing in the tone touches the card.  It is a 15-bit counter and two muxes
on the audio wires; what it does is add about 170 LUTs and move the
placement.  The SD and floppy paths are the ones that cannot survive that,
for a reason progress.md already records: sd_img_mounted[0..3], disk_step,
fdd/clk_dsk and ram1/curs_set are flops clocked by DATA signals, and
test003.sdc constrains them at an invented 1 us.  The timing report says
zero setup violations about paths it never honestly analysed, so
place-and-route can break them silently and the report stays green.  That
is the fragility progress.md warns about, in the sentence "None of this is
broken today.  All of it is why the design is fragile against
re-place-and-route."

The tone had done its job - it proved the sink played nothing even from a
known-good bipolar signal, which is what led to the cadence - so it is
removed rather than defended.  git has it if it is wanted again.
