lets add hdmi audio also

****

Done, and it was bigger than it sounds, because Gowin's DVI_TX IP is what
its name says.

The finding first.  DVI_TX is DVI: video only, no data islands, so no
audio.  Its own documentation offers exactly two options - external clock
and OBUF type - and there is no Gowin HDMI IP with audio anywhere in the
Education install.  So HDMI audio was not a setting to turn on; it meant
replacing the IP.

src/hdmi/, four files:

  hdmi_tx.v       timing, packet scheduling, audio capture, packet picker
  tmds_channel.v  8b/10b, control words, TERC4, both guard bands
  hdmi_packet.v   the 32-clock packet and its BCH ECC
  hdmi_serdes.v   rPLL x5, four OSER10, four ELVDS_OBUF

The two coding files are transcriptions of HDMI 1.4a sections 5.4 and
5.2.3.4, derived from Sameer Puri's hdl-util/hdmi (MIT) and rewritten in
Verilog-2001.  The rest is ours: upstream's top level generates its own
video timing, and sdram2.v already does that here.

Three decisions worth knowing.  The scheduler takes its timing entirely
from de/hs/vs and knows no mode - a data island has to be announced eight
clocks ahead and a video period ten, which means knowing the future, so
the video goes through a 12-deep delay line and the scheduler reads the
undelayed signals.  The island sits in the back porch, four packets a
line, on blanking lines as well as active ones, which is what carries the
audio across the frame boundary.  And the HDMI audio is resampled at
clkpix/1024 rather than taken off the I2S path: at 50.143 MHz that is
48.968 kHz, and the exactness is the point - HDMI wants Fs = f_TMDS x N /
(128 x CTS), so CTS = 8N holds whatever the clock really is, and N = 6144
with CTS = 49152 are constants rather than a measurement.  The sink
regenerates the audio clock from them and plays at the true rate.

The I2S output is untouched.

Checked, and more than usual, because a bad encoder shows up as no picture
rather than no sound:

- make lint clean.
- The testbench now decodes the TMDS stream the way a sink does - follow
  the preambles and guard bands into a video or data island period and
  decode what is inside.  So make frames no longer taps the RGB signals;
  it rebuilds the picture out of the encoder's own output, and it still
  reads СТАРТОВЫЙ ТЕСТ / - ошибка ОЗУ ЦП at 1280x600.  That is a round
  trip through the 8b/10b, not an inspection of its input.
- Over a 1.4 s run: 177876 packets, 0 ECC errors, 69482 audio samples, no
  overflow.  With +AUDIOTEST forcing two different constants onto the AY
  mix, 2976 of 2995 audio samples came back non-zero with L != R - the
  same answer the I2S monitor gives, from the other output.
- make bitstream places it.  0 setup violations; Logic 39% -> 41%,
  Register 23%, BSRAM 48% unchanged, PLL still 2 of 2 - the second one
  used to be inside dvi_tx doing the same job.  The tool derives the
  serial clock on its own as a generated 250.715 MHz off the pixel clock,
  which is what makes the OSER10s analysable at all, and nothing under
  src/hdmi/ appears in the ten worst timing paths.  The tightest path is
  still sdram2's horz counter through the OSD into I_rgb_*, at 1.82 ns;
  it got slower because the die got fuller, not because anything was
  added to it.

Not checked: no display has ever been asked to play this.  Two things I
left out deliberately and either could be why a real sink stays silent -
there is no General Control Packet, and the AVI InfoFrame carries VIC 0
because 1280x600 at 50.7 Hz is not a CEA mode.  Those are the first two
things to try.  The ECC check in the testbench uses the same polynomial
constant as the encoder, so it proves the plumbing - bit order, timing,
reset - and not the polynomial.

ip/dvi_tx is still in the .gprj and instantiated nowhere, deliberately: it
is the fallback, and putting it back is one instance in top.v.

Also changed: tools/srcs.py grew a STUBBED list so hdmi_serdes.v joins the
vendor IP in being replaced by a simulation model - it is nothing but
Gowin primitives, and a 250 MHz serial clock would have cost the whole run
to watch bits the encoder has already chosen.  Docs updated in CLAUDE.md,
.claude/docs/{fpga,video,build,progress}.md, tang/src/test003.sdc's header
and both howto files.
